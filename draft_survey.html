Method & formulas

1. Mark mean drafts: F = (Fp+Fs)/2; A = (Ap+As)/2; M = (Mp+Ms)/2

2. Convert to perpendicular drafts using mark positions from AP. Stem corrections applied.

3. Quarter mean draft (hog/sag): D = (TF + TA + 6·TM) / 8

4. 2D Hydrostatic interpolation: DISP, TPC, MCT, LCF read from 6 trim tables (−1.5 to +1.0 m) using bilinear interpolation in (draft, trim).

5. First trim correction: FTC = TR × LCFmid × TPC × 100 / LBP

6. Second trim correction: STC = TR² × 50 × d(MTC)/dz / LBP

7. Density correction: W = (DISP + FTC + STC) × ρdock / 1.025

8. Ballast tanks: bilinear interpolation in (sounding × trim). Mass = volume × ρballast.

9. Cargo = ΔW − ΔDeductibles. Lightship and Constant cancel out.
